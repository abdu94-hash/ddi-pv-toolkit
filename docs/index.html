Companion to the book

Drug–Drug Interactions in Drug Safety and Pharmacovigilance

The book's worked calculations as interactive teaching tools. Each calculator opens with the book's example; change the inputs to see how the answer moves, and read the assumption that comes with it.

For learning only. These are simplified static models with illustrative or fictional values. They do not predict what happens in an individual patient, and they must not be used for dosing or any other clinical decision. Ravolixan and Meridian Therapeutics are fictional.

    Formulas, inputs, and valid ranges: spec/CALCULATORS.md. The same calculations are tested against the book's numbers in spec/vectors.json and are available as a Python package and command-line tool in python/.

    Educational use only: simplified static models from the book; not for dosing or clinical decisions.
