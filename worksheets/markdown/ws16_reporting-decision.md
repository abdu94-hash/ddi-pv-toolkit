# Worksheet 16: Reporting decision

*Practice output for Chapter 16, Seriousness, expectedness, and reporting decisions.*

**Annotation.** The worksheet records the document and its version beside every expectedness judgment, so that a later reviewer can reproduce the decision. The coding of hypokalemia and hypomagnesemia as events assumes that the investigator reported them as adverse events; if they were reported only as results, they belong in the test results section (Chapter 15).
