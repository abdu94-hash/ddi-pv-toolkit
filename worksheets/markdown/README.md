# Worksheets

The blank worksheets that close each chapter of *Drug–Drug Interactions in Drug Safety and Pharmacovigilance* (H. Selim), one per chapter. They are the same worksheets as the book's Appendix A, the companion workbook. Each chapter shows a completed example with annotations.

Each worksheet comes in two formats:

- **Word** (`../docx/`): editable US Letter files with the book's styling and room to write. Worksheets with five or more columns are landscape.
- **Markdown** (this folder): the same tables, for reading on GitHub or pasting into other tools.

The worksheets are teaching tools that make reasoning visible. They are not validated instruments, none of them produces a score or a probability of causation, and they must not be used for dosing or clinical decisions. Adapt the wording to your organization's procedures.
